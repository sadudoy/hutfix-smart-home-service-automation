Terms of Service

Welcome to HutFix. These Terms of Service constitute a legally binding agreement between you and HutFix Technologies Ltd., governing your access to and use of our website, mobile interface, and matchmaking dispatch services.

1. Platform Scope & Marketplace Agency

HutFix operates an online communications and booking platform connecting residential and commercial clients with independent service providers and certified technicians across Dhaka. HutFix facilitates quotes, booking requests, scheduling, dispatch tracking, and payment processing.

2. User Accounts & Responsibilities

To book or provide home services, you must register for an account using verified credentials. You agree to provide accurate, current, and complete information, including a valid Bangladeshi phone number and Dhaka physical service address. You are responsible for safeguarding your password and account security.

3. Pricing, Quotations & Billing

All standard base fees, hourly diagnostic charges, and emergency surcharges are quoted in Bangladeshi Taka (৳). Additional parts, consumables, or structural hardware required during a repair must be agreed upon between the customer and provider before installation. Payment can be settled via Cash on Delivery (COD) or verified digital methods.

4. Prohibited Conduct

Users agree not to: (a) solicit or negotiate off-platform transactions to evade platform commission; (b) harass, threaten, or abuse service technicians or support staff; (c) misrepresent service requirements or false identities; or (d) post fraudulent reviews or falsify job records.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your personal data when you interact with HutFix.

1. Information We Collect

  • Identity Data: Full name, phone number, email address, and profile photo.
  • Location & Address Data: Service location coordinates, apartment details, and neighborhood designations in Dhaka.
  • Transaction & Service Logs: Booking history, diagnostic notes, technician ratings, and dispute records.
  • Technician Background Info: National Identity Card (NID) numbers, trade certificates, and banking/payout details.

2. How We Utilize Your Data

We use your data strictly to execute requested services: dispatching nearby certified repairmen, rendering real-time tracking routes, sending SMS/push notifications on booking progress, maintaining platform safety, and resolving support inquiries.

3. Data Security & Storage

All sensitive account records are stored in secured database environments with industry-standard bcrypt encryption for credentials and HTTPS/TLS encryption for all communications. We do not sell or monetize personal data with third-party advertising brokers.

Refund Policy

Customer Guarantee: If a service is not completed to your satisfaction, please raise a dispute within 24 hours for a full review.

1. 24-Hour Dispute Resolution Window

We stand behind the quality of services completed through HutFix. If a technician leaves a job unfinished, causes accidental property damage, or fails to resolve the agreed malfunction, notify our team within 24 hours of job completion by opening a Support Ticket or tapping "Raise Dispute" in your booking history.

2. Refund Eligibility & Remedies

  • Complete Refund: In cases of technician no-show, improper diagnostic execution resulting in no service, or verified substandard work that requires complete redo by an alternate specialist.
  • Free Re-Service: If an appliance repair displays symptoms of the original fault within 7 days, HutFix will dispatch a senior technician for warranty reinspection at zero additional service charge.
  • Dispute Arbitration: Our operations team reviews technician logs, diagnostic photos, and customer feedback. Validated refunds are returned via the original payment channel within 3 to 5 business days.

Trust & Safety

Strict Verification Standard: Every provider on HutFix undergoes an NID verification process before being approved by our admin team.

1. Multi-Stage Provider Screening

Safety and reliability begin before a technician ever takes their first job. Our vetting protocol includes:

  • National Identity Verification: Government NID authenticity verification through authorized databases to confirm legal identity.
  • Skills & Certification Assessment: Practical evaluation of trade experience (HVAC, electrical wiring, plumbing systems, appliance mechanics).
  • Code of Conduct Training: Professional ethics, customer privacy standards, and hygienic home-service protocols.

2. In-Home Safety Protocols

When a specialist arrives at your doorstep, they must display their digital HutFix ID badge matching their photo and profile in your app. Customers can track their technician's arrival live, share the trip link with family members, and reach our emergency helpline at any point during an ongoing visit.

3. Zero Tolerance for Fraud

HutFix maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy for harassment, dishonesty, or substandard craftsmanship. Technicians with ratings falling below 4.0 or accounts involved in confirmed security breaches are permanently deactivated and banned from the platform.
